Fourteen quick-reply packs maintained by the AMEFYS team plus the player community: essentials, tactical commands, classic banter, number-memes, timings, Russian, SEA — and the new zh / en / ru taunt series. Download → import → tap a chip in the overlay.

Download any .amefys-replies.json.
2

3
Click Import and pick the JSON file.
4
In the overlay → Quick Replies → tap chips to send.
